How it works

Three steps to live

No infrastructure knowledge needed. The bot handles everything.

01

Upload your file

Send your Python bot file to HostBot on Telegram. We accept .py files up to 100 MB.

02

Admin reviews

An admin approves your bot. Once approved, your file gets stored on R2 and queued for deployment.

03

Bot goes live

A Docker container spins up with your bot. Monitor logs, restart, or stop anytime from the Telegram interface.
